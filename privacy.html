Amalnama - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Amalnama is a free personal routine and worship checklist web app available at https://uowyeasin-cyber.github.io/AmalnamaForever/. It has no server and no database of its own.

Data the app accesses

Only if you choose "Connect with Google", the app asks for two permissions:

  • Google Calendar events - to create, update and delete the reminder events that you add in the app.
  • Google Drive app files (drive.file) - to save one file named Amalnama-data.json in your own Drive so your laptop and phone stay in sync. The app can only see files it created itself.

Where your data is stored

Your routine, checklist and settings are stored in your own browser and, if connected, in your own Google Drive and Google Calendar. The developer never receives, sees, sells or shares your data. No analytics, tracking or advertising is used.

Access tokens

Google access tokens stay only in your browser session and expire after about one hour.

Removing your data

You can disconnect at any time with the button in the app or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Delete Amalnama-data.json from your Drive and the Amalnama events from your Calendar to remove all data.
