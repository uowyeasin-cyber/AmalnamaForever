# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Amalnama's community to be a respectful, welcoming place for everyone, in the spirit of good character (*akhlaq*). We pledge to make participation free from harassment for everyone, regardless of age, background, ability, nationality, or level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

* Speak kindly and assume good intentions.
* Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it gently.
* Respect differences of opinion, including between schools of thought (madhhabs).
* Focus on what is best for the users of the app.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Insults, mockery, personal or sectarian attacks, takfir.
* Harassment, threats, or publishing others' private information.
* Spam, advertising, or content that is sexual, violent, or hateful.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening a confidential [security advisory](https://github.com/uowyeasin-cyber/AmalnamaForever/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and ban participants who break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
