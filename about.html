What Amalnama does

Daily checklistTick off the five daily prayers, classes, study sessions and personal tasks, and see your weekly and monthly progress.
Routine and remindersBuild your own weekly routine. If you connect Google, each item is added to your Google Calendar with the reminder times you choose.
QuranRead all 114 surahs with a translation in your language and listen to each ayah or the full surah.
Azan and prayer timesAutomatic prayer times for your city and an azan at each prayer while the app is open.
Private communityPosts, group chat, private chats, voice and video calls. New members join only after the administrator approves them. Shared media is deleted after 30 days.
6 languagesবাংলা, English, Bahasa Melayu, العربية, اردو and Kiswahili.

How Amalnama uses your Google data

Connecting Google is optional. The app works without it and keeps your data on your device. If you choose Connect with Google, Amalnama asks only for Drive app files (for sync). Calendar reminders are a separate, optional permission you can turn on later:

PermissionWhy it is needed
View and edit events on all your calendars (Google Calendar events, optional)To create, update and delete the reminder events for the routine items, exams and events that you add in Amalnama. The app only changes events it created.
See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this appTo save one file, Amalnama-data.json, in your own Drive so your phone and laptop stay in sync.

Your Google data goes directly between your device and Google. It is never sent to the developer, sold, shared, or used for advertising. Amalnama's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Free, open and ad-free

Amalnama is made by Yeasin Arafat, a student developer, as a free tool for the community. There are no ads, no tracking and no paid features.
